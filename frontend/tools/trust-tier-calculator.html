How fees are distributed

Every platform fee is split three ways: 40% is retained by the platform for operational costs, 30% flows to the treasury (funding governance operations and constitutional infrastructure), and 30% goes to the mission pool (rewarding future mission completion). This split is constant across all trust tiers — only the fee rate itself changes.
