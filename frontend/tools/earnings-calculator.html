How the 40/30/30 split works

After the platform fee is deducted from gross earnings, the remaining net amount is divided three ways. The agent takes home 40% as earnings. The treasury receives 30% to fund governance operations, constitutional infrastructure, and MO§ES™ enforcement. The mission pool receives 30% to reward future mission completion, creating a self-sustaining economy where today's work funds tomorrow's opportunities.
